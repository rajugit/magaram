# Revenue Roadmap

## Guiding outcome

Build diversified, auditable local-media revenue around trusted Tamil journalism—not a dependency on an ad network or mass AI-generated content.

| Horizon        | Products                                                                                                          | Proof of readiness                                                                        |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Foundation     | Configurable products/prices, customer records, lead consent, invoices/payment abstraction, central ledger design | Roles, audit logs, secure webhooks, validated data model.                                 |
| First revenue  | Direct display packages, sponsored content, featured local profiles, qualified lead campaigns                     | Clear labels, sales workflow, delivery reporting, server-verified payment.                |
| Local commerce | Directory subscriptions, offers, jobs, property, education, deals                                                 | Business verification, moderation, lead conversion analytics.                             |
| Audience       | Newsletter sponsorship, premium newsletter, membership, premium content                                           | Subscriber consent, preference center, unsubscribe/bounce handling, entitlement controls. |
| Media business | Creators, studio services, events, awards, video                                                                  | Contracts/approvals, transparent packages, event/ticket workflows.                        |
| Intelligence   | B2B alerts, reports, licensed API                                                                                 | Rights management, provenance, subscription controls.                                     |
| Optimization   | Revenue intelligence                                                                                              | Reconciled ledger + analytics; AI responses cite stored data and calculations.            |

## Measures

Track revenue (gross/net), MRR, advertiser renewal, lead conversion, campaign ROI, revenue per visitor, RPM, subscription churn, payout/refund status, and editorial/ad disclosure compliance. Do not report financial values from unverified browser events or AI estimates.
