# Magaram editorial QA

Work from the Magaram repository's `docs/EDITORIAL_ACCEPTANCE.md` and the supplied draft/source material. This skill is maintained at `skills/magaram-editorial-qa/SKILL.md`. Keep Tamil reader-facing language natural and specific to the actual place and event. Use India time for reader-facing dates. Do not substitute a guessed district, quotation, source, allegation or statistic for missing evidence.

For journalism, distinguish the reported fact, named source, time of verification, disputed claim and editorial opinion. Confirm current or disputed facts against supplied primary sources or research them when the task authorizes research. Flag unsupported claims for the editor; do not invent a publishable replacement. Protect private source/contact data from public article projections.

For product changes, preserve source verification, independent approval, draft privacy and visible corrections. AI proposals remain proposals until accepted through the human workflow. Business listings and offers are separate from editorial endorsements; paid material needs a visible sponsorship label. Offer eligibility, dates, terms and enquiry consent must match the stored records and actual provider capability.

Test both Tamil copy and the workflow that enforces it. Report concrete defects with a proposed correction, and identify any unresolved evidence. Publishing, contacting a source, or sending a message requires the user's existing task authorization; reading this skill does not grant it.
